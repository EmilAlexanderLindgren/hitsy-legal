Hitsy

Music Party Game for iPhone and iPad.
Hör låten. Gissa året.

Download on the App Store

Free to download. Available in English and Swedish.

How it works

  1. A song plays. You don't see the title, the artist or the year.
  2. Place its card on your timeline, before or after the cards you already have.
  3. Flip it. A correct placement keeps the card; a wrong one doesn't.
  4. The first player to place ten cards correctly wins.

Play your way

  • Pass and play: 2–6 players around one phone.
  • Local multiplayer: play on each other's devices over Multipeer Connectivity, with no account and no sign-up.
  • Card packs: Hits, Summer Bops, Party Anthems, Guilty Pleasures, Karaoke Bangers, Britpop, Europop, Film & TV and more, each with its own look.
  • Dagens Hitsy: one new challenge a day, the same songs for everyone, with a result you can share.
  • Abilities: earn stars from bonus challenges and spend them on a clue or a skip.

Music

Hitsy plays songs through Apple Music. With an Apple Music subscription you hear full songs; without one you hear 30-second previews. Every card has a link to open the song in Apple Music. Apple Music is a trademark of Apple Inc.
